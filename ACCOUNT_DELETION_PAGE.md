# Delete Your Desi Tracker Account

**Desi Tracker** lets you delete your account and associated data at any time.

## Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)
1. Open the **Desi Tracker** app and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile** (or **Settings**).
3. Tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm. Your account and associated data are permanently removed.

## Option 2 — Request deletion by email
If you can't access the app, email **info.desitracker@gmail.com** from the email
address on your account with the subject **"Delete my account"**. We will verify
your identity and delete your account within 30 days.

## What data is deleted
When you delete your account, we permanently delete or anonymise:
- Your name, email address, phone number, and password
- Your profile and any photos you uploaded
- Your role and account settings
- For business accounts: your business listings, products, orders and related records

## What data may be retained
We may retain limited records where required by law or for legitimate accounting/
fraud-prevention purposes (for example, transaction records) for up to the period
required by applicable law, after which they are deleted.
